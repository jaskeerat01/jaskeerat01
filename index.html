01

Profile

I work with open sources, and I care about what can honestly be concluded from them.

I’m a computer science student at Guru Tegh Bahadur Institute of Technology (GGSIPU), reading B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with a CGPA of 8.755. My focus sits in cybersecurity research — open-source intelligence, threat intelligence and breach analysis.

My interest in this field started with a personal experience of cyber fraud. Understanding how information gets collected, exposed and turned into risk gave me a reason to focus on the defensive side, and it still shapes how I work: careful investigation, evidence-based analysis, ethical research, and findings written up clearly enough for somebody else to act on.

The material I publish comes from public sources, CTF exercises and my own analysis of publicly reported incidents. I would rather publish a small finding I can defend than a large one I cannot.

Areas of interest

The subjects I am actively studying and practising. This is a map of where my attention is going — not a claim of professional specialisation in each one.

OSINT & digital footprints

What is publicly discoverable about an organisation or an individual, which sources agree with each other, and where the picture stops being reliable.

Threat intelligence

Following emerging threats and reading how reported incidents actually unfold, rather than how they are described.

Dark web monitoring

Watching relevant spaces for information that may matter to defenders, and working out what is worth escalating.

Breach analysis

Working through published incident reporting and separating what was claimed from what was actually confirmed.

IOC extraction & classification

Pulling indicators of compromise out of raw material and describing what each one actually represents.

Network & web security

Nmap, Wireshark and Burp Suite — and the fundamentals behind what those tools are showing me.

Linux & security labs

Practical exercises in environments built to be probed, because that is where the understanding comes from.

Research automation

Using Python and n8n to take the repetitive parts of research and monitoring off my hands.

Documentation & community

Writing up findings clearly, reviewing code, and helping other contributors move forward.
